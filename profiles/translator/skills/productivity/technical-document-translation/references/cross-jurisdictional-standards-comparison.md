# Cross-jurisdictional engineering standards comparison

Use for questions comparing Russian, Chinese, or other national engineering/environmental standards.

## Method

1. Identify the exact regulated object before comparing numbers. Terms that look equivalent may cover different physical layers or scopes (for example, Russian `плодородный слой почвы`, Chinese `耕作层`/plow layer, and `表土层`/surface soil).
2. Establish the hierarchy in each jurisdiction: national law/standard, industry standard, provincial/regional standard, project specification. Do not present a provincial figure as a universal national rule.
3. Prefer primary or official sources. Record standard number, title, status, issuing body, date, and scope. Use an official standards platform for metadata and the standard text/PDF for technical clauses.
4. Compare the decision method, not only the headline number: field survey, soil/profile classification, laboratory thresholds, maps/contours, machinery constraints, cost, and destination/use of removed material.
5. Separate three concepts explicitly: definition of the layer; design thickness/depth; construction pass thickness. A 30 cm maximum per pass is not necessarily a 30 cm total removal depth.
6. Build a compact comparison table: regulated object, basis of determination, typical/range values, exclusions, project deliverable, and approval hierarchy.
7. State whether values are mandatory, recommended (`宜`), or merely typical (`一般`). Preserve normative modality in translation.
8. End with a project rule: the host-country standard and site investigation control unless the contract or authority imposes a stricter compatible requirement.

## Translation cautions

- `耕作层` = пахотный/обрабатываемый слой, not automatically the full fertile profile.
- `表土层` = поверхностный слой почвы/topsoil; it may include more than the cultivated horizon.
- `肥沃土层` can describe a fertile layer but must not replace a defined regulatory term without checking the source standard.
- `剥离厚度` = thickness/depth of stripping; distinguish total design thickness from thickness per machine pass.

## Verification

- Cite each jurisdiction separately and attach clauses to the claims they support.
- Cross-check numerical ranges against the standard table or clause, not search snippets.
- If the text layer scrambles a table, render the page and transcribe the relevant row visually.
- Disclose unresolved differences in terminology, status, or regional applicability; never harmonize them by assumption.
